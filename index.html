About

I build at the intersection of technology, artificial intelligence and visual creativity.

Somewhere in that process, I got more interested in the model doing the thinking than the chart doing the reporting, and moved into machine learning: regression, classification, and computer vision systems that had to work in the real world, not just a notebook.
