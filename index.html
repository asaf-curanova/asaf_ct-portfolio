    About

    From dashboards to diffusion models.

    I started out where most data careers do — cleaning spreadsheets, building Power BI dashboards, and wiring up cloud pipelines. Somewhere in that process, I got more interested in the model doing the thinking than the chart doing the reporting, and moved into machine learning: regression and classification problems, then computer vision and OCR systems that had to work in the real world, not just a notebook.

    In parallel, I picked up an unusual second track — directing generative AI image and video output, prompt by prompt, for brand and creative projects. It's a different kind of precision: instead of tuning a loss function, you're tuning a prompt until a character stays consistent across twenty frames.

    Right now, both tracks meet in healthcare technology, where I'm working on AI/ML initiatives and Hospital Information System development. I like problems where the constraint is real — a hospital workflow, a noisy image, a dataset that doesn't behave — and the solution has to actually ship.
